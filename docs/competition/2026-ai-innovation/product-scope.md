# Product scope

Product capabilities are developed once in GaitLogic Planner and remain product capabilities after the competition: state modelling, knowledge-model structure, rule-engine framework, agent-tool contracts, plan validation, and dynamic-adjustment framework. The competition is an evaluation and presentation context, not a second product line.

AI outputs remain training-plan drafts. Users must confirm before applying a draft, and the product does not provide medical diagnosis or replace a coach or clinician.
